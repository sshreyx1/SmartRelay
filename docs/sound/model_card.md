Generated by `ml/train.py` on 2026-11-03 from `data/features_v1.csv` (grid: full). Do not edit by hand.

Features the trees may not use (still computed on the node): loud_std, loud_range, log_rms, active_frac.

## What it does

Runs on the Pico after every capture: 25 streaming spectral features (`firmware/sensing/sound_features.c`, v1), then **stage 1** picks the sound group and, for an animal group, **stage 2** picks the animal. A stage-2 label is kept only when its confidence is at least **101%** (`SOUND_STAGE2_MIN_CONF`); otherwise the node reports just the group. The backend only displays the result.

## Limitations

- ESC-50 is not Singapore wildlife: these are farm and pet animals and generic birds/insects (Phase B fixes this).
- Played through a speaker in a room is not the field: distance, wind and overlapping calls are not covered.
- The MAX9814's AGC rescales everything, so loudness features (`loud_*`, `log_rms`) carry little information.
- ESC-50 is licensed CC BY-NC 3.0 (Piczak 2015); fine for coursework, not for a commercial product.

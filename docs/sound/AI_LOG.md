| 2026-11-03 | Plan A3: int16 Q15 FFT with a fixed >>1 per stage (1/256 overall). | A quiet frame (a few ADC counts, i.e. ambient) comes out of a fixed-scale 8-stage integer FFT as mostly rounding noise.
